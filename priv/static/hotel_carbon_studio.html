Hotel Carbon Studio

A bottom-up model of one hotel's operational footprint. Turn the knobs on the left; the board recomputes per occupied room-night, per guest-night and per square metre, sliced by amenity, scope or energy carrier, against the benchmarks the graph verified. Defaults reproduce a New York luxury hotel of about 375 keys; the base building is split into CBECS 2018 lodging end uses so each lever acts on its own slice. Scenarios can be saved for everyone who opens the page.

NYC Local Law 97

Hotel (R-1) limits 0.00987 tCO2e/ft² for 2024–29 (106.2 kg/m²) and 0.00526 for 2030–34 (56.6 kg/m²); penalty $268 per tCO2e over the limit. Uses the LL97 coefficients (electricity 0.289 then 0.145 kg/kWh; gas 0.181 kg/kWh; steam 0.147 kg/kWh) regardless of the factor chosen above. Scope 3 is outside LL97.
