Hotel Carbon Storylines

Compose a story: a hotel, a grid and policy path, and dated events (retrofits, programmes, shocks). The simulator runs 2025–2040 with the effect ranges the evidence gives, draws hundreds of paths, and shows where the footprint, the LL97 position and the penalty exposure land. Put stories side by side, then write down what you believe and let the stories argue.
