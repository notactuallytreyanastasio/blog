Where one night's carbon comes from, and whose books it lands in.

Three carriers on the left (electricity from the grid, gas or steam burned for heat, and things the hotel buys), eighteen slices of the building in the middle, the three GHG Protocol scopes on the right. II

The road to 2040 runs over a cliff in 2030.

Local Law 97 caps a hotel at 106 kg CO₂e per m² until 2029 and 57 from 2030, then 28 and 16, at $268 for every tonne over. The band is two hundred Monte Carlo runs of your plan, each drawing every lever's effect from its measured low–high range and the grid within ±15%. The dashed line is doing nothing. Drag the plan's dots to move a measure in time; drag the brass cursor or click any chart to change the year.

      V

      What the hotel may say in 2025.

      The year on the console also sets the law. From 27 September 2026 the EU bans neutrality claims built on offsets bought outside the value chain; courts in the Netherlands and Germany got there first. LL97 does not care what is claimed, only what is burned. Open a claim for the reasoning.
