Building and Deploying This Blog

Introduction

I didn't want to have an Ecto dependency here because I am serving static content. So, I made the app LiveView but didn't add Ecto at all. I have vendored the posts in to priv and read them all out from there with a primitive tagging system.

I will be building some more features on top of that later, but let's cover this blog as a whole.
